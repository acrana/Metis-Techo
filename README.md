## 🚀 Featured Core Platforms

### [METIS / AICDSS (Clinical Decision Support System)](https://github.com/acrana/Metis-Techo/tree/main/AICDSS)
An intelligent clinical decision support application designed to assist healthcare providers with real-time risk assessment and medication safety protocols.
- **Stack:** Python, PySide6, SQLite, PyTorch, Pandas
- **Highlights:** Real-time patient monitoring, medication contraindication checks, and a responsive dark-mode desktop UI.

### [CLABSI Risk Prediction Pipeline](https://github.com/acrana/Metis-Techo/tree/main/CLABSI)
An explainable machine learning pipeline utilizing the MIMIC-IV critical care database to predict Central Line-Associated Bloodstream Infection (CLABSI) risk.
- **Stack:** Python, Scikit-Learn, XGBoost, MIMIC-IV (PostgreSQL)
- **Highlights:** Optimized XGBoost classifier (87% accuracy, 0.70 ROC-AUC) trained on cleaned clinical data and invasive line characteristics.

## 🔬 Advanced Specialty Projects

### [Themis-Kansa](https://github.com/acrana/Themis-Kansa) *(Clinical AI Governance & Audit)*
- **Focus:** AI Model Validation & Fairness Auditing
- **Description:** Evaluates ICU scoring and mortality models (such as SAPS II) on MIMIC-IV data to simulate a hospital AI governance committee audit. Tests for demographic parity, calibration (Brier scores), discrimination, and temporal drift.

### [Panacea-Shogo](https://github.com/acrana/Panacea-Shogo) *(EHR Interoperability)*
- **Focus:** SMART on FHIR Application & Medication Reconciliation
- **Description:** A secure web-launchable SMART on FHIR application utilizing OAuth2 authentication and the RxNav API to ingest, normalize, and reconcile patient medication lists.

### [Hermes-Kakehashi](https://github.com/acrana/Hermes-Kakehashi) *(Cross-Border Terminology Bridge)*
- **Focus:** International Health Data & Regulatory Mapping
- **Description:** A specialized translation and compliance tool mapping US RxNorm active ingredients to Japanese pharmaceutical nomenclature (YJ codes), while auditing traveler import restrictions.

## 💡 Interactive Clinical Simulations

### [Neurocritical Care CO₂ Simulator](https://github.com/acrana)
- **Focus:** Cerebral Hemodynamic Modeling & Education
- **Description:** An interactive clinical simulation tool modeling real-time cerebral blood flow (CBF), cerebral perfusion pressure (CPP), and ICP waveform compliance (P1, P2, P3 peaks) under varying arterial CO₂ and MAP conditions.
